How to use it

1Open this same website on both phones.

2On phone A, choose Send, select files, and create the offer.

3Copy the offer to phone B. Choose Receive, paste it, and create the answer.

4Copy the answer back to phone A and tap Connect and send.

Tip: manual connection text can be long, so use a trusted messaging app or copy/paste method. Never share private files with people you don't trust. Large transfers may be limited by browser memory and connection quality.
